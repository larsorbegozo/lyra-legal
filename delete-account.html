Delete your Lyra account

Effective 27 November 2026

You can delete your Lyra account and all the study data stored for it, in the app or by writing to us. Lyra is run by Laurencio Jerónimo Orbegozo.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Lyra, go to Cuenta and tap Borrar cuenta.
  2. Choose whether to keep your progress on this phone or erase it too.
  3. Tap Enviar código, type the 6-digit code we email you, and tap Borrar cuenta.

Your account and your answers are deleted from our server immediately. This cannot be undone.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, write to lars@aurenstudio.co with the subject Delete my Lyra account, from the email address of the account. If you write from another address we may ask you to prove that the account is yours. We delete the account and answers within 5 business days and confirm by email.

What is deleted, and what is not

  • Deleted: your account, your email address, every study answer stored on our server, your starting level, and the record of the terms you accepted.
  • Not affected: what is saved on your phone. It stays until you erase it in the deletion flow, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes it.
  • Kept for a short time: encrypted backups and request logs at our hosting provider disappear in its normal cycle, and copies of the sign-in emails we sent you stay in our mailbox until you ask us to delete them. We do not restore deleted accounts.
