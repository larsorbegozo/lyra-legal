Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026

In short:

You can study without an account, and then your answers stay on your phone. If you create an account, we keep your email address and your study answers on a server so you can recover them on another phone. We do not run ads, we do not sell data, and we do not use analytics or tracking tools. You can delete your account and its data at any time from the app.

1. Who is responsible

Laurencio Jerónimo Orbegozo, an individual based in Argentina (“we”), is responsible for the personal data described here. You can reach us at lars@aurenstudio.co.

What we do not collect: your name, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera, advertising ID, or any payment information. Lyra contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. If that ever changes, we will update this policy first and tell you in the app.

3. Why we use it

  • To run your account: let you sign in and recover your progress on another phone.
  • To send you the sign-in code by email.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse, for example by limiting how many codes can be requested.

We rely on your consent when you create an account and on our need to provide the service you asked for. We do not use your data to profile you or to decide anything about you automatically.

4. Who receives your data

  • Supabase hosts our database and the sign-in system. Account data and answers are stored on its servers in the United States (US East).
  • Zoho Mail delivers the sign-in emails. It sees your email address and the code we send.
  • Google Play distributes the app. Google handles your Play account and download data under its own policy; we do not receive your personal data from it.

We do not sell your data and do not share it with anyone else, except if the law requires us to.

5. Transfers to other countries

Because Supabase stores data in the United States, your data leaves your country if you create an account. We rely on our provider’s data processing terms and security measures for this. If you never sign in, your answers never leave your phone.

6. How long we keep it

  • On your phone: until you erase it (when you delete your account you can choose to erase it too) or uninstall the app.
  • On our server: until you delete your account. Deleting your account from the app removes your account, all your answers and the record of what you accepted from the server immediately.
  • Backups and logs: our hosting provider may keep encrypted backups and request logs for a limited time. We do not restore deleted accounts, and these copies disappear in the provider’s normal cycle.
  • Sign-in emails: copies of the emails we sent you stay in our mailbox until we delete them. Ask us and we will delete them.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, update or delete your personal data. Under Argentine law (Ley 25.326), you can access your data free of charge at intervals of at least six months, and we answer an access request within 10 calendar days and a correction or deletion request within 5 business days. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you also have the rights of the GDPR, including data portability, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

The fastest way to delete everything is inside the app: Home → Cuenta → Borrar cuenta. Without the app, follow the steps on Delete your account, or write to lars@aurenstudio.co.

8. Children

Lyra is for people aged 13 and over and is not designed for or aimed at children. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 13, we will delete it. If you think that has happened, write to lars@aurenstudio.co.

9. Security

Data travels encrypted. Each account can read only its own answers, enforced by the database itself, and there are no passwords to leak. No system is perfectly secure, so we also keep what we collect to a minimum.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before the change applies and show the new effective date at the top of this page.
